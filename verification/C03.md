## Delivered

- A second task pack for directed unit-cost minimum hop distances, bounded to
  1..128 vertices and at most 16384 directed pairs. Loops, cycles, duplicates and
  unreachable vertices belong to the domain; weighted edges and other contracts
  are explicitly unsupported.
- Three task-relative mechanism families and four complete-module rules,
  including a recognizable FIFO implementation with an incorrect increment.
  Graph sources use the same passive intake, review, assignment, receipt and
  reporting path as sorting. Existing sorting pack bytes and rule identities
  remain unchanged.
- Study-bound comparison templates with unknown metadata left blank, explicit
  fixed-prefix collection positions, retained missing/failed observations and
  disclosed later exclusions. No filename-derived chronology or success backfill.
- Condition, task, evidence-policy, budget and sampling checks; separate finite
  validity scope checks. Incompatible inputs retain inventories with reasons;
  malformed or stale bindings fail. Numeric differences include their exact
  fractions, populations and coverage.
- A designed graph example, actual project-owned finite receipts, JSON/Markdown
  output and the command/field guide in `docs/C03.md`.

The finite oracle uses independently written all-pairs intermediate-vertex
closure. Only the explicitly reviewed project-owned reference strings and their
presentation variants are executed in development. Production comparison and
inspection never call that oracle or execute a candidate.

The classification material is a designed software corpus, with reference,
renamed and misleading-comment variants plus uncovered forms, opaque calls,
inactive code, a hybrid, a single-pass solver, a wrong entry point and invalid
syntax. It is not a held-out sample of model-generated programs. Conditional
accuracy on these cases does not establish generalization.

remains applicable, with the original count regression rerun as part of the suite.

## Observable comparison

The left prefix contains four observations: FIFO=2, SETTLE=1, RELAX=1. All pass
the supplied ten-case suite. All-classified and valid-classified SCI are 3/8.

The right prefix has observations at positions 1, 2 and 4, all classified FIFO.
Position 3 is explicitly missing. Position 2 fails six tests. The later SETTLE
observation at position 5 is retained in the inventory and excluded from the
prefix. The right all-classified population is 3 and valid-classified population
is 2; both have SCI=1. Observed passing is 2/3, and observed pass coverage over
requested slots is 2/4. Missingness is not recoded as a failed test.

Both descriptive SCI differences are 5/8; support differences are -2 and
Gini-Simpson differences are -5/8. Those are conditional fixture calculations.
The configuration names, collection design and budget are stipulated, and the
same source appears in both prefixes. No real model difference, independent
sampling or causal effect is established.

Comparison fault cases cover missing accounting, omitted later records, duplicate
positions/revisions, foreign configurations, stale study/task bindings, unknown
controls, changed budgets, cross-task/material mismatch, changed test suites or
environments, absent receipts, timeout/partial outcomes, provisional labels and
empty prefixes. These checks support the stated boundaries; no general mutation
score is claimed.
